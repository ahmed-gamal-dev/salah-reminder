# Store listing drafts / نصوص المتاجر

## Desktop — English

**Name:** Salah Reminder

**Summary:** Offline prayer times, next-prayer countdown and configurable adhkar reminders.

Calculate daily prayer times offline with multiple calculation methods, Asr schools and manual adjustments. Choose your city, enter coordinates, or optionally detect an approximate location. Receive configurable prayer and adhkar reminders, with optional adhan or your own sound. Arabic RTL and English interfaces, tray controls and optional login startup are included. On supported MacBooks, an optional notch panel shows the next prayer and countdown; hover for today's times, and stop adhan playback from the expanded panel.

Prayer times may differ from a local mosque or authority; use manual adjustments when needed. Features available in a sandboxed store build must be confirmed before using this copy for that build.

## Chrome / Firefox — English

**Name:** Salah Reminder · مواقيت الصلاة

**Summary:** Offline prayer times, next-prayer countdown, notifications and adhkar reminders in Arabic and English.

Choose a city or enter coordinates, select your calculation method and Asr school, and view today's prayer times and the next-prayer countdown. Set prayer and morning/evening/after-prayer adhkar notifications. Read selected adhkar and track repetitions. Calculations run locally; no account, advertisements, analytics or browsing-history access. Notifications require the browser to be running and enabled by the operating system. The extension does not provide the macOS notch, computer locking or the desktop app's adhan audio player.
